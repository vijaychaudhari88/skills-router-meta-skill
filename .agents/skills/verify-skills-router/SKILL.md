## Invariants Formally Verified

1. **Strict Determinism & Purity**:
   $$\forall q \in \text{Queries}, \forall s \in \text{State}: \text{route}(q, s) \equiv \text{route}(q, s)$$
   Routing evaluation performs zero hidden mutations and guarantees idempotent ordering.

2. **Valid Decision Triad Partitioning**:
   $$\text{Decision}(q) \in \{\text{'route'}, \text{'ambiguous'}, \text{'no_route'}\}$$
   Every query strictly resolves to one of the 3 triad states. A `route` always yields a non-empty actionable skill set; a `no_route` never hallucinates a skill match.

3. **Profile Monotonicity & Dormancy Isolation**:
   $$\text{Skills}(P) \subseteq \text{Skills}(\text{'all'})$$
   Dormant skills (filtered out by profile) can never be routed under any input query.

4. **Pipeline 4-Phase Soundness**:
   Every route emits all 4 required operational phases (`Phase 1 (Guardrails)`, `Phase 2 (Pre-Execution)`, `Phase 3 (Execution)`, `Phase 4 (Verification)`).
